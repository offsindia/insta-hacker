Privacy policy

A starter policy for review and customization before the website is published.

Information we collect

The current website is a static demonstration. The contact form does not send or store submissions. If a backend is later connected, list the exact information collected and why.

How information is used

Use submitted information only for responding to enquiries, providing the requested authorized service, preventing abuse, and meeting applicable legal obligations.

Credentials and sensitive information

Do not request or store passwords, OTPs, recovery codes, session cookies, payment PINs, or unnecessary private content.

Payments

Payments must be handled by an approved provider. Do not store raw card details. Verify payments on the server using the provider's documented signatures and webhooks.

Retention and security

Document a retention schedule, deletion process, staff access controls, encryption, backups, and incident response plan before accepting customer data.

Contact and rights

Add the real business identity and a monitored privacy contact, and explain how users can request access, correction, deletion, or grievance handling as applicable.

This template is not legal advice. Customize it to actual data flows, vendors, business identity, and applicable law with qualified review.
