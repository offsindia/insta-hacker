Account access & protection

Get back to your account. The right way.

Help with official recovery routes, business account configuration, admin permissions, and compromised-account response across major platforms.

What we can help you do

  • Navigate official account recovery and identity verification steps.
  • Secure an account after suspected compromise, including session review and recovery-email checks.
  • Set up business/creator accounts and configure legitimate team access roles.
  • Review privacy, login alerts, multi-factor authentication, and impersonation reporting.
  • Prepare a clear support case when a platform's automated recovery process fails.

Platforms covered

Meta: Instagram, Facebook, Messenger, and Meta business assets. Messaging: WhatsApp and Telegram. Video & creator: YouTube and TikTok. Professional: LinkedIn. Other social networks: X, Snapchat, Threads, Pinterest, and Reddit.

Each platform controls its own identity checks and access decisions. We provide guidance and authorized setup; we cannot guarantee recovery or override a platform's security decisions.

What to prepare

  • Proof that you own or are authorized to manage the account.
  • Access to the registered email or phone, if still available.
  • Approximate date the issue began and any platform case/reference number.
Never share your password, OTP, backup codes, session cookies, or remote-control access with anyone claiming they can “hack it back”.
