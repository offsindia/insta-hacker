Incident response

Preserve the facts. Protect the evidence.

Practical guidance for authorized audio and file review, online scam documentation, and organizing evidence for a responsible report.

Audio and file review

We can help define a workflow for files you lawfully possess: organize originals, note timestamps and context, prepare summaries, and avoid accidental alteration. Any actual file-processing service must be explicitly scoped and securely implemented.

Call and message scam documentation

  • Record dates, displayed caller IDs, and relevant message screenshots.
  • Preserve transaction references and official notifications.
  • Keep original evidence in a restricted-access location.
  • Report through the relevant telecom provider, platform, bank, or official cybercrime channel.

Call details and voice recordings

Call-detail records are controlled by telecom providers and applicable legal processes. We can guide users toward their own account records and lawful reporting routes, but cannot retrieve another person's private call history from a phone number alone or secretly activate a microphone.

Incident response

  • Secure affected accounts from a trusted device.
  • Change reused passwords and enable MFA.
  • Revoke unfamiliar sessions and preserve alerts.
  • Document what happened and when, then report through appropriate channels.
